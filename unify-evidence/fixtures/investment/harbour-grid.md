# Harbour Grid: synthetic diligence note
This fictional case was authored for this demonstration in September 2026.
Harbour Grid develops a 24 MW data centre in the fictional district of Eastmere.
The grid connection is conditional on substation approval, with energisation planned for Q3 2028.

## Delivery risk
The contractor has not secured a fixed-price transformer order.
A six-month delay in grid connection would defer contracted revenue, requiring a larger liquidity reserve.
Next diligence action: obtain the utility milestone schedule and identify termination rights.

## Commercial evidence
The anchor tenant represents 58 percent of contracted capacity.
Contracted capacity and recognised revenue are different measures and must not be conflated.
