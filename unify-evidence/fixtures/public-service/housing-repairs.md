# Fenwick housing repairs: synthetic service briefing
Fenwick is a fictional local authority. All figures in this file are invented for testing.
Housing repairs received 420 requests in the demonstration month.
Urgent heating failures had a median triage time of 11 hours.

## Service design
Duplicate housing repairs reports arrive through both telephone and online forms.
Residents without internet access must retain a telephone route.
The proposed pilot links repeat reports to an existing case while preserving the original contact record.

## Pilot evaluation
Measure repeat-contact rate, time to first appointment, and missed urgent cases.
Do not infer pilot success solely from a reduction in total recorded requests.
